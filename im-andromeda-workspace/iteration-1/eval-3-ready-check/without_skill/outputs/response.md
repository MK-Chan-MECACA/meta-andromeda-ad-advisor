# Andromeda — Ready Check

Yes. I'm ready to judge your ads.

I'm Andromeda — the retrieval-and-matching lens on your Meta setup. I don't guess from vibes. I look at what you give me, map it to how Meta's delivery system actually retrieves, matches, and learns, and tell you what's working, what's starving the algorithm, and what to fix first.

**I won't issue a PASS/FAIL verdict until you send a specific ad.** No creative, no setup, no diagnosis — only readiness.

---

## What I need from you

Send as much of the following as you have. More detail = sharper judgment.

### 1. Creative (the ticket)

- **Format:** video, static, carousel, etc. — aspect ratio if relevant
- **Hook / opening:** first 1–3 seconds (video) or headline (static)
- **Primary text:** full copy
- **Headline & description:** as shown in the ad
- **Visual description:** what's on screen — people, product, text overlays, UGC vs polished, etc.
- **CTA button:** e.g. Shop Now, Learn More, Enquiry Now, Send Message
- **Concept count:** how many *distinct* creative ideas you have (not just hook-text variants of one video)

### 2. Campaign setup

- **Objective:** Purchase, Leads, Traffic, etc.
- **Conversion location / destination:** website, messaging app, instant form, etc.
- **Performance goal:** what Meta is optimizing for (e.g. Maximize conversions, Maximize conversations)
- **Budget & schedule:** daily/lifetime, amount, start date, any end date
- **Placements:** Advantage+ or manual selection
- **Audience:** geo, age, gender, language, detailed targeting (or broad/empty)
- **Structure:** number of campaigns, ad sets, ads — and how they're split (interests, lookalikes, broad, etc.)

### 3. Tracking & signals

- **Pixel / CAPI status:** pixel only, CAPI, both, neither
- **Conversion event:** Purchase, Lead, Complete Registration, custom event, etc.
- **Any catalog / product feed details** if relevant

### 4. Business context

- **What you're selling** and to whom
- **Price point / offer** (if applicable)
- **Geo & language**
- **Any constraints:** brand rules, compliance, seasonality, launch deadlines

### 5. Performance data (if live)

- **Spend, impressions, CTR, CPC, CPM**
- **Conversion volume & cost** (CPA, CPL, ROAS — whatever matches your objective)
- **Frequency, reach**
- **How long the ad/set/campaign has been running**
- **Any changes you've made** (pausing rules, budget shifts, creative swaps)

If the ad isn't live yet, say so. I'll mark live performance as **Unknown** and judge setup + creative readiness instead.

---

## What you'll get back

Once you send the above, I'll return a structured review:

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Verdict** | Clear read — aligned, at risk, or misaligned — with no fake certainty |
| **Stage 0** | Objective, offer, and funnel fit |
| **Stage 1 — Retrieval** | Whether your structure and targeting help or hurt Meta's ability to find buyers |
| **Stage 2 — Matching** | Whether your tickets (creatives) give the system real diversity to match |
| **Learning** | Whether budget, events, and optimization choices let the algorithm learn |
| **Scorecard** | Quick strengths / weaknesses |
| **Fixes** | Prioritized, concrete changes — not generic "test more audiences" advice |

## How to send it

Paste everything in one message. A screenshot description works if you can't export copy. If you have multiple ads, send them one at a time or label each clearly.
