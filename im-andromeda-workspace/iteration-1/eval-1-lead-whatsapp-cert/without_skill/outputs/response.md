## Stage 1 Retrieval — What I Match This Ticket To

When I retrieve, I read the **whole surface**: image text, face, badges, primary copy, headline, CTA, and page context. Here is what your single ticket is competing on:

### Retrieval signals (strong)
- **"Automate 80% of your work"** — productivity pain, office worker, SME operator
- **Dr. Lau Cher Han + 15,000+ professionals + 46 cohorts** — authority, social proof
- **HRD Corp / HRDC claimable badges** — employer budget, L&D buyer, Malaysian corporate
- **"No coding" / "beginner-friendly" / n8n** — tool-curious non-technical professional
- **"Bring one annoying office task"** — concrete, low-friction workshop promise
- **WhatsApp CTA** — high-intent, conversational, mobile-native in MY

### Retrieval conflicts (same ticket, mixed intents)
Your static tries to be **two ads in one frame**:
1. Left hook = **personal productivity / AI fear-of-missing-out**
2. Right side + primary text = **employer-funded HRDC certification**

I will match both — but inconsistently. Some impressions go to individuals who want free AI tips, not a RM-paid cert. Some go to HR managers who ignore "80% of YOUR work" because they buy for teams, not selves.

That is not fatal with broad targeting. It **is** fatal when you only have **one ticket** and no variant to split those intents.

### Ticket diversity score: **1 / 5 minimum viable**

You described:
- 1 static 1:1
- 1 hook ("WHAT IF YOU COULD AUTOMATE 80%...")
- 1 visual concept (split text + instructor photo)
- 6 benefit bullets repeated in image **and** primary text (redundant, not diverse)

Twelve hook swaps of the same layout would still be **one ticket**. I need **different concepts**, not different bullet order:
